## 1. How to clean a Puffco Hot Knife

**Slug:** `how-to-clean-puffco-hot-knife`

**Distinct job:** Remove residue from an electronic tool's ceramic tip without treating the whole tool like a removable glass accessory. Existing model guides do not own this accessory.

**Search evidence:** The exact question returns official support, product FAQs and retailer recommendations. [Zee Vapor's current FAQ](https://www.zeevapor.com/2026/08/is-puffco-hot-knife-worth-it/) advises warming first; the newer official instructions say to cool completely. That disagreement gives this page a concrete reason to exist.

**Answer and outline:** Let it cool fully. Clean only the ceramic tip gently with an ISO-damp cotton swab, keep liquid away from electrical components and charging ports, and wipe the body with a dry microfiber cloth. Do not present scraping, whole-tool soaking or reheating as cleaning shortcuts. Explain the boundary between cleaning a dirty tip and repairing a broken one. Link to support for a damaged tip rather than recommending more supplies.

**Primary source:** [Puffco: How do I clean my Hot Knife?](https://puffco.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/45897875194907-How-do-I-clean-my-Hot-Knife), updated January 26, 2026. That page does not prescribe a numeric alcohol concentration; do not invent one for this tool.

**Amazon category:** Ordinary cotton swabs; optional plain microfiber cloth. Reader may already own both. Do not link an Amazon Hot Knife listing: Puffco's support states it does not authorize marketplace resellers.

**Picture value:** High for tip-versus-body identification. Use an authorized product photo or a clearly labeled explanatory diagram; do not fake a before/after test. Internal links: swab comparison and cleaning kit checklist.

## 3. How long should a Puffco dry after cleaning?

**Slug:** `how-long-puffco-dry-after-cleaning`

**Distinct job:** Decide whether cleaned parts can be reassembled; separate soak time from drying time. Do not repeat each model's disassembly instructions.

**Search evidence:** Search results include [Proxy drying-time questions](https://www.reddit.com/r/puffco/comments/1ulx6vi/how_long_to_let_proxy_chamber_air_dry_for_after/) and [retailer connection-error guides](https://e-nail.com/blog/puffco-peak-pro-chamber-not-detected-redwhite-lights-fix-guide/) giving different hour/minute rules. The thread is evidence of the question, not a source for the suggested methods.

**Answer and outline:** There is no universal timer in the cited manufacturer instructions: the requirement is complete air drying. A 20-minute soak is not a promise of 20-minute total turnaround. Give a short readiness checklist: remove and separate the allowed parts, follow glass-rinse versus chamber-no-water rules, place them securely for air drying, keep the base and port dry, inspect accessible recesses and contacts, and wait longer whenever moisture remains or dryness is uncertain. Absence of visible droplets or smell is not proof that an enclosed chamber is dry. Do not prescribe a heat cycle, hair dryer, microwave or flame to rush the process. Do not promise a particular drying rack makes it safe sooner.

**Primary sources:** [Current Proxy manual](https://puffco.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/45365020389275), [New Peak care](https://www.puffco.com/pages/new-peak-support). Both require complete drying; the Proxy manual prohibits a water rinse for the chamber and rinsing/submerging the base.

**Amazon category:** Usually none necessary. A plain clean towel or stable drying mat is optional surface protection, not proof of drying or model compatibility.

**Picture value:** A clearly labeled glass/chamber/base separation diagram would help more than a stock beauty photo. Internal links: model chooser; alcohol guide.

## 4. Why glass stays cloudy after cleaning

**Slug:** `cloudy-glass-after-cleaning`

**Distinct job:** Troubleshoot remaining film or spots after cleaning rather than repeat the full wash routine. Scope is detached glass, never a powered base or ceramic chamber.

**Search evidence:** `Puffco glass water spots` yields numerous recurring questions such as [this water-mark question](https://www.reddit.com/r/puffco/comments/1bc4fk3); the search results commonly prescribe stronger chemicals without distinguishing the part. Glassmaker care FAQs address mineral deposits and surface damage separately.

**Answer and outline:** A cloudy appearance alone does not identify its cause. Ask what was cleaned, which product was used, whether the mark is an oily film, droplet-shaped deposit or persistent rough-looking haze. Start with the maker's permitted routine and thorough rinse where allowed. Explain that mineral deposits and surface damage are different from residue, and repeated aggressive scrubbing is not a universal fix. RIEDEL permits vinegar for superficial marks on its own glasses; that does **not** authorize vinegar in Puffco glass. Puffco Proxy instructions explicitly exclude other cleaners and abrasives. Refer persistent marks to the actual glass maker; stop using chipped or cracked pieces. Never identify mold or declare a piece safe from a photo.

**Primary sources:** [RIEDEL care FAQ](https://www.riedel.com/en-int/information-legal/frequently-asked-questions), [Puffco Proxy glass care](https://www.puffco.com/pages/proxy-support), [Corning inspection infographic](https://www.corning.com/catalog/cls/documents/infographics/CLS-GL-063.pdf) for general glass inspection—not its industrial cleaning chemistry.

**Amazon category:** No universal stain-remover recommendation. A plain microfiber cloth can help with an accessible exterior if permitted. Do not sell vinegar or industrial descaler as a Puffco-approved fix.

**Picture value:** High only if actual labeled examples exist. Do not generate diagnostic “before/after” photos. A symptom/next-step table is useful without photos. Internal links: drying guide and applicable device guide.

## 5. How to choose a cleaning brush for narrow glass

**Slug:** `cleaning-brush-for-narrow-glass`

**Distinct job:** Choose a tool that physically reaches an approved-to-brush removable glass opening. Different from swab comparison and full glass washing.

**Search evidence:** Search results for brush/narrow glass questions include [generic affiliate brush rankings](https://www.journeymanhq.com/523020/best-brush-cleaners/) and broad marketplace sets, while [OXO's set](https://www.oxo.com/water-bottle-cleaning-set.html) identifies different brush jobs and dimensions. The useful gap is a fit checklist, not another unsourced “best” ranking.

**Answer and outline:** Identify whether the maker permits brushing first. Measure the smallest accessible opening and needed reach; a long handle does not mean a wide brush head fits. Compare bottle, straw and detail brush shapes. Check bristle coverage and tip/end construction; avoid exposed metal and stop if insertion needs force. Do not push into blind bends, enclosed percolators or electronics. Inspect wear after washing and let the brush dry separately. Manufacturer-published overall dimensions are not necessarily bristle diameter; do not promise fit from them. Example OXO set has nylon bristles and separate bottle/straw/detail brushes, but is not tested in Dab Pal or any Puffco.

**Primary sources:** [OXO exact brush set](https://www.oxo.com/water-bottle-cleaning-set.html); [Corning product selection guide, cleaning section](https://www.corning.com/catalog/cls/documents/selection-guides/CLS-GL-001.pdf), which warns against worn brushes exposing the spine. Do not transpose its industrial acid-cleaning methods. Puffco Proxy's no-abrasives guidance remains controlling for its glass.

**Amazon category:** Nylon bottle/straw/detail brush set, matched to the exact OXO model if available. Avoid claiming “scratch-proof.” An approved soak without a brush is a valid alternative.

**Picture value:** Very high: exact authorized brush set image or diagram showing opening, head width and reach. Internal links: cloudy glass and kit checklist.

## 6. How to clean a silicone dab mat

**Slug:** `how-to-clean-silicone-dab-mat`

**Distinct job:** Wash a removable mat after spills; do not expand into another cleaning-station shopping list.

**Search evidence:** Current results include several [Oil Slick care articles](https://oilslickpad.com/blogs/news/how-to-clean-dab-tools-rigs-pads-safely) and generic store advice. The useful distinction is plain silicone versus printed, fabric-backed or mixed-material mats. Even maker blog posts disagree about ISO, so do not offer a universal solvent soak.

**Answer and outline:** Confirm the mat is plain silicone with compatible care instructions; remove all gear and let it cool. Remove loose debris gently, rinse with warm water, use a small amount of mild dish soap and a soft sponge or fingertips, rinse thoroughly and dry fully before replacing equipment. No metal scraping, abrasive pads, blanket dishwasher advice, or promise to restore a permanently damaged print. Treat remaining sticky patches according to that exact maker's instructions. Keep alcohol-bottle claims separate from mat care.

**Primary sources:** [Oil Slick's mat-care section](https://oilslickpad.com/blogs/news/how-to-clean-dab-tools-rigs-pads-safely). [Eyce's silicone rig instructions](https://www.eyce.com/pages/eyce-rig-user-guide) are a useful counterexample: they specify mild soap/water and reject alcohol for that product. Do not claim all silicone behaves identically. Oil Slick's other posts contradict each other on ISO; use the conservative soap-and-water procedure, not its solvent claims or minute-based evaporation claim.

**Amazon category:** Soft nonabrasive sponge or plain cloth if needed. No need to recommend a replacement mat when the reader just needs to wash one.

**Picture value:** Moderate: actual mat/material close-up or care-label picture. No fake stain-removal proof. Internal links: kit checklist and brush guide when relevant.

## 7. How to clean terp pearls without treating every material alike

**Slug:** `how-to-clean-terp-pearls`

**Distinct job:** Identify a tiny removable accessory's material and clean it without thermal shock or losing it; not advice on consuming or improving effects.

**Search evidence:** [Roots Glass's current pearl guide](https://rootsglass.com/terp-pearls/) includes a cleaning section and generic soak times, while gemstone makers publish more specific thermal-shock warnings. Device-specific ceramic pearls have different instructions. Comparison table should clarify this mismatch rather than rank materials.

**Answer and outline:** Identify ceramic, quartz, ruby/sapphire or unknown material from the order record, not color. Let the part cool before solvent contact. For a Proxy Core ceramic pearl, Puffco permits removing and soaking it in 90%+ ISO; keep the Core cup out of the bath. For Ruby Pearl Co gemstones, the product page prohibits cleaner on hot stones and direct flame; it does not supply a universal cold-soak duration. Avoid inventing a cross-material 20–30-minute recipe. Use a stable shallow work area so pieces cannot roll into the sink; pick up cooled pieces carefully without clamping force. Fully dry per maker before returning the part. If unknown or chipped, stop and confirm material/replacement with the seller.

**Primary sources:** [Puffco Proxy/Core support](https://www.puffco.com/pages/proxy-support); [Ruby Pearl Co 6mm ruby product care](https://www.rubypearlco.com/products/6mm-ruby-pearls). Do not repeat the latter's unproven performance superlatives.

**Amazon category:** Cotton swabs; a small-parts handling tool only if its tip and dimensions are verified. No random marketplace gemstone recommendation.

**Picture value:** High for identifying the exact item, but color cannot establish material. Label exact product/material and source; do not label generic red beads “ruby.” Internal links: quartz guide and Proxy guide.

**Answer and outline:** The eChamber is swab-cleaned, **never submerged in ISO or any liquid**. Distinguish the maker's dry-swab routine and allowed ISO-damp swab from a bath. Remove the glass and Quick Connect Adapter for separate cleaning; wipe residue around the eChamber/adapter with a swab or paper towel as directed. Glass alone can receive the separate ISO cleaning, water rinse and air dry. Let all parts fully air dry before use. Do not dry-burn an eChamber as a cleaning trick. Explain that persistent staining does not automatically mean failure; a cracked dish is a stop/replacement issue. Link official troubleshooting rather than promising cleaning will fix every fault.

**Primary source:** [Boost Evo knowledge base](https://drdabber.helpscoutdocs.com/article/119-boost-evo-knowledge-base), especially “Cleaning your Boost Evo.” Its current cleaning paragraph does not specify an exact ISO percentage; do not silently import Switch²'s value.
