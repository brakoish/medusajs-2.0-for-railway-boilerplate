## Publication boundary

Do not scrape or rehost an Amazon listing image unless the allowed Amazon content mechanism and its conditions are established. Do not interpret a public manufacturer gallery as a commercial photo license. No express manufacturer grant for Q-tips, Glob Mops, or Puffco was established in the earlier pilot research.

For **exact linked-product images**, an authorized Amazon image source, explicit supplier permission, or original photography is still the right route. CC photos can add useful editorial context now, but none above honestly supplies the current Q-tips Original 750 × 3 or Glob Mops XL 2.0 300-count product photo. Keep that distinction visible in any final completion claim.
