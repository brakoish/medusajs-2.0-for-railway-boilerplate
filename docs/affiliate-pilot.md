## Reader and measurement safeguards

- Disclosure below the introduction and beside the shopping block; every affiliate anchor says “Amazon (paid link)” and uses `rel="sponsored noopener"`.
- Affiliate anchors work without JavaScript. The small client click handler never prevents navigation.
- `affiliate_click` uses article slug, public ASIN and fixed placement only. It uses the existing production-host and opt-in analytics gate. No visitor-specific Amazon tag, Amazon script, customer email or order information.
- Existing `$pageview` and `guide_product_click` provide reach and own-product interest. Amazon Associates reports remain the source for qualifying orders and commission earnings. A click is not a sale.
- Updated privacy copy describes external Amazon attribution. Essential-only visitors can still read and follow links.
- Core cup care is distinguished from soakable Proxy parts; the blanket Proxy HowTo object and misleading 30-minute total were removed.
