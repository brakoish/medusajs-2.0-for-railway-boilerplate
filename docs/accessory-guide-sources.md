# Accessory and Dr. Dabber guide sources

Research checked 2026-09-24. Five new articles live in `storefront/src/modules/blog/new-guides-accessories.ts`. No firsthand testing is claimed. These are maintenance guides, not instructions for substance use.

## Silicone dab mat

- https://oilslickpad.com/blogs/news/how-to-clean-dab-tools-rigs-pads-safely — silicone section: mild soap, warm water, soft sponge, thorough rinse, dry; plastic/silicone scraper; brief ISO wipe is not a long soak; avoid aggressive tools/heat. The article does not adopt unrelated warm-solvent advice elsewhere on that page.
- https://dulytek.com/products/dulytek-rosin-silicone-pad — soap/water and hang to dry.
- https://honeybeeherb.com/products/dabbing-mat — current page verified again: soap/warm water and complete drying only. Earlier indexed text described alcohol spot-wiping; that claim was removed after current-source validation.
- Distinction: all-silicone versus fabric/printed/backed products. No universal alcohol/dishwasher permission. Optional microfiber only; ordinary household cloth is sufficient.

## Terp pearls

- https://yodabbadabba.com/dabbing-resources/how-to-clean-dab-rig-nails-accessories/ — quartz accessory ISO cleaning and soft wiping. We do not transfer the source's heated-tool advice to pearls.
- https://www.rubypearlco.com/products/6mm-ruby-pearls — no hot gemstone/liquid contact or direct flame.
- https://rubiesareforever.com/pages/insert-care-guide — ruby/sapphire guidance also applies to pearls; ISO only for unburned residue and completely cool gems; burned deposits require its separate method.
- https://ritual-co.com/pages/ritual-ruby-shop — ruby pearls completely cool before any liquid, fully dry after; no direct flame.
- No common soak time or cleaner promised across materials. Unknown/coated/opal pearls excluded. Practical tray/counting/storage steps are editorial handling advice. Optional ordinary cotton swabs; no claimed product performance ranking.

## Glass carb cap

- Current Honeybee Hive and Dream Bubble pages no longer substantiate cleaning times, concentrations or salt prohibitions found in earlier indexed versions. Removed both sources and all dependent specifics from the article after fresh verification. No universal soak duration is asserted.
- https://puffco.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055108754-How-do-I-clean-my-Peak — original Peak glass carb cap ISO soak, 99% preferred, rinse/dry. Explicitly not generalized to newer multi-material cap assemblies.
- No soak permission for magnets, glued decoration or attached silicone inferred. Ordinary swabs and ISO offered only within approved glass-care scope.

## Switch 2

- https://drdabber.helpscoutdocs.com/article/126-switch2-knowledge-base — primary current source: 91%+ internal/glass ISO cleaning; never ISO on outer Blue Onyx shell; glass removal from bottom, not mouthpiece; glass before crown; avoid insert holes when gripping; no hot insert removal; no torch cleaning. All parts fully air-dry.
- https://drdabber.helpscoutdocs.com/article/127-switch2-user-manual — manufacturer manual link for reader reference. Instruction manual text independently read in its PDF at https://tools420.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Dr.-Dabber-Switch-2-Manual.pdf confirms tweezers, no hot removal, routine swabbing in place, 99% for glass and dish soap/damp towel for shell. Current knowledge base supplies updated internal/glass concentration and removal directions.
- Preserves Switch 2 / original Switch / Switch GO distinction. No induction cleaning cycle temperature interpretation or unsupported fixed drying time.
- Routine insert cleaning rechecked against official KB's Keeping it Clean section: dry swab after use, then ISO-damp swab while still warm. Article explicitly stops heating/turns off first, keeps insert installed for routine swabbing, and allows removal only when necessary and never hot.

## Boost Evo

- https://drdabber.helpscoutdocs.com/article/119-boost-evo-knowledge-base — dry swab after use; ISO-damp swab for warm chamber; wipe chamber/adapter; glass separately ISO clean then water rinse/air-dry; **never soak eChamber in any liquid**; no dry burn; different fault instructions by serial range.
- https://drdabber.helpscoutdocs.com/article/112-boost-evo-user-manual — manufacturer manual image directly opened and visually read: https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5abd26ed2c7d3a0e9366db52/images/63d1b8ec7eb7a63c30e3f4f1/file-vZVyIaR8f7.jpg . Specifies up to91% for atomizer/glass and up to75% for QCA/base, neither atomizer/QCA/base submerged. Article preserves those limits.
- Current KB has no concentration but linked printed manual does. Only cotton swabs offered; no ISO99 product on this guide. No dose, operating temperature or device-use instruction included.
